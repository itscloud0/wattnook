- Refuse cut/restore at ≤20% battery; a reserve block clears only above 25%.
- Positive explicit sensor freshness bound; missing, stale, future-dated, nonfinite or implausible data cannot initiate a cut. Temperature plausibility for this policy is 0–100°C inclusive.
- Faults latch. A known/possibly owned hold receives at most one restoration attempt, not a repeated command every sample. This is not a guarantee that restoration succeeded.
- Requested and observed states remain distinct. The future synchronous caller must supply completed verified readback on the next invocation; a disagreement faults. This policy has no asynchronous acknowledgement/timeout contract and must not be wired to one without extending it.

`RecoveryAttempted` is an additional state field to bound fault recovery. `LastTransition` records command issuance conservatively, not physical-transition proof. A new supervised controller session/reconciliation, not toggling Enabled, is required to reset a fault. No policy state alone establishes actual charging or thermal safety.
