THESIS: A clear energy overview, not a stack of similarly weighted buttons. Headline state, charge limit, directional energy, actions and secondary telemetry read in that order.

OWN-WORLD: Actual Liquid Glass with restrained blue selection/energy, warm warning only for real problems, SF Pro and tabular readings, 24-point margins, one 4-point rhythm, spacious native destinations and quiet secondary actions.

STORY: Read charge and temperature, understand source/destination and watts, change limit or Keep Awake deliberately, inspect System/Storage without duplicate app rows.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 400×532-point shell. Header and full-width Battery/System/Storage navigation; Battery's unboxed percentage/temperature then explicit limit and preserved animated ribbon. Two action lanes, three summary metrics and SSD occupancy below. System owns aligned three-column values/context and six consumer rows. Settings groups Appearance, Menu bar, Monitoring and utilities; History retains charts on its own page. Signature interaction is the existing visible-only, Reduce-Motion-aware traveling energy highlight.

FORM: Transit wayfinding, grounded candidate 5. Its material becomes the user's pinned Glass, never a literal map; typography, density and stable information lanes transfer. Legacy-tool limitation: the installed concept-seed version emitted a real local roll but no durable SEED REFERENCE. No verifiable reference is claimed or fabricated. Original evidence: `/private/tmp/wattnook-redesign-seed-network.txt`; assignment and catalog judgments are recorded in `docs/menubar-full-redesign.md`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Storage uses the existing 400×450 pt page, explicit scanning, Applications/App caches segments, name search, real app icons and 44 pt native rows.
