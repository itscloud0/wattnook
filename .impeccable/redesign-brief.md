## Direction contract

THESIS: A clear energy overview, not a stack of similarly weighted buttons. Headline state, charge limit, directional energy, actions and secondary telemetry read in that order.

OWN-WORLD: Actual Liquid Glass with restrained blue selection/energy, warm warning only for real problems, SF Pro and tabular readings, 24-point margins, one 4-point rhythm, spacious native destinations and quiet secondary actions.

STORY: Read charge and temperature, understand source/destination and watts, change limit or Keep Awake deliberately, inspect System/Storage without duplicate app rows.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 400×548-point shell. Header and full-width Battery/System navigation; Battery's unboxed percentage/temperature then explicit limit and preserved animated ribbon. Two action lanes, three summary metrics and SSD occupancy below. System owns aligned three-column values/context and six consumer rows. Settings groups Appearance, Menu bar, Monitoring and utilities; History retains charts on its own page. Signature interaction is the existing visible-only, Reduce-Motion-aware traveling energy highlight.

FORM: Transit wayfinding, grounded candidate 5. Its material becomes the user's pinned Glass, never a literal map; typography, density and stable information lanes transfer. Seed: none (the installed concept-seed version printed no durable SEED REFERENCE; local roll key remains in the local roll record).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
